Privacy Policy

Power2lift (the "App") is provided by an individual developer. This page explains how the App handles information.

Your training records

Everything you enter in the App, including training records (weights, reps, RPE, notes, exercise names and programs) and body logs (body weight, sleep and so on), is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server. The App has no account or login.

The share and export features pass content to an app you choose, such as Mail, only when you use them yourself.

Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services to diagnose problems, understand usage and show ads. The contents of your training and body logs are never sent to them, and the App does not collect information that identifies you personally, such as your name or email address.

  • Firebase Analytics (usage): screens opened, counts and types of actions such as completed sets, device information (OS, model), app version, approximate region, and an app instance identifier
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting): diagnostic information when a crash occurs (crash logs, device information, OS, app version)
  • Google AdMob (ads): the device advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID), device information, approximate location, and ad interactions such as impressions and clicks
  • RevenueCat (managing the Pro purchase): purchase information (plan and expiry) and an anonymous app user identifier. The App contacts RevenueCat only when you open the purchase sheet, restore purchases, or launch the App after purchasing

Ads are shown only after you have logged training on a certain number of days. Until then, the ad service is not started and no ad-related network requests are made.

Firebase and AdMob are Google services, and the information they collect is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Information handled by RevenueCat is subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Payments are processed by Apple; the App never receives your payment details.

Tracking and ad personalization

On iOS, once you become eligible for ads, the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission once. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used and non-personalized ads are shown. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, the App asks for ad consent, and no ads are loaded there until consent is given.

Opting out and deleting data

To stop ad-related data collection, turn off tracking or ad personalization in your device settings. Uninstalling the App deletes all training and body logs stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

For questions or feedback about the App, please use this form. What you enter in the form, including your email address if you provide one, is used only to reply to you and to improve the App.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
