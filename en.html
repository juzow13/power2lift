Power2lift

Open it, and today's session is there. One tap to log a set.

Power2lift is a training log built for powerlifters. Plan your weeks, work through today's session, and see whether you are getting stronger. It does that one thing, and it is built to be used one-handed in the gym.

  • Open it, trainToday's session is on screen the moment you launch. No login, no tutorial.
  • Logging is one tapIf the set went as planned, tap it. Edit weight, reps or RPE only when something was different. Mis-taps can be undone.
  • Backoff weights, calculatedPlan backoff sets as a percentage of your top set and the weight is worked out for you, rounded down to 2.5 kg.
  • Blocks planned from your TrMAXBlock, week, day, exercise, set. Periodization lives in the structure. Sessions run in order, not on weekdays, so a missed day just pushes the plan back.
  • Last time, always in viewThe set you are on shows what you did on the same exercise last time.
  • See whether you are progressingPer-exercise e1RM and volume over time, with your PRs.
  • Your records stay on your phoneNo account. Your training records are stored only on your device, and every feature works offline.
